# Sales Performance & Regional Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance, customer growth, and regional profitability using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard brings together monthly sales, target figures, customer volumes, profit by region, and performance completion indicators into a single management-friendly view.

## Business Objective

The objective was to provide a clear view of sales and customer performance over time and identify regional patterns in profitability.

The analysis focuses on:

* Tracking monthly sales performance
* Comparing sales with recorded targets
* Monitoring customer growth
* Evaluating profit across regions
* Presenting key performance indicators through an interactive dashboard

## Dataset

The dataset contains sales activity recorded across seven regions from January to September 2023.

The analysis includes:

* Month
* Region
* Sales
* Profit
* Target Sales
* Customers
* Quarter
* Sales Completion Rate
* Profit Completion Rate
* Customer Completion Rate

## Sales Performance

![Sales Performance](screenshots/02-sales-performance.png)

Sales increased substantially across the period, rising from 30,000 in January to a peak of 130,000 in August.

July and August represented the strongest sales months, while January recorded the lowest sales volume.

## Customer Growth

![Customer Growth](screenshots/03-customer-growth.png)

Customer volume increased from 300 in January to 2,000 in September.

The strongest customer growth occurred during the second half of the period, with customer volume reaching 1,600 in June and 1,800 in July before reaching the period high of 2,000 in September.

## Regional Profitability

![Regional Profitability](screenshots/04-regional-profitability.png)

Brazil recorded the highest regional profit at 129,875, followed by Chile at 128,833 and Peru at 127,340.

Profit was relatively evenly distributed across the regions, with only a 3,895 difference between the highest and lowest regional profit figures.

## Key Insights

1. Sales increased significantly throughout the period, reaching a peak of 130,000 in August.
2. Customer volume grew substantially, from 300 customers in January to 2,000 in September.
3. The strongest customer volume occurred in September, despite August recording the highest sales.
4. Brazil generated the highest recorded regional profit at 129,875.
5. Regional profit was relatively balanced, with no single region accounting for a disproportionately large share of total profit.
6. The second half of the period showed stronger overall sales and customer activity than the first half.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management could:

* Investigate the factors driving the significant increase in sales during the second half of the period.
* Examine the customer acquisition or retention activities associated with the increase in customer volume.
* Identify the practices contributing to Brazil's leading profitability and assess whether they can be replicated in other regions.
* Continue monitoring regional profitability to identify changes in performance over time.
* Review the recorded target figures to ensure the target methodology is consistent with the sales data before using the figures for formal performance measurement.
