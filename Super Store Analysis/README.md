# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses a Superstore transactional dataset using **Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, and an interactive dashboard**. The analysis transforms raw order-level data into actionable business insights across sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regions, and shipping modes.

## Business Objective

The objective is to evaluate overall business performance, identify the main drivers of sales and profit, uncover underperforming products and regions, and translate the findings into practical recommendations for improving profitability and decision-making.

The analysis focuses on:

* Measuring sales, profit, order volume, and profit margin
* Tracking performance trends over time
* Comparing regional and customer-segment performance
* Evaluating category and sub-category profitability
* Identifying loss-making products and potential discount-related risks
* Supporting management decisions with clear, data-driven recommendations

## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard provides a high-level view of business performance and allows key trends and performance differences to be interpreted quickly.

### Performance Highlights

* Sales increased from **$479.7K in 2014** to **$733.2K in 2017**.
* Profit increased from **$49.5K in 2014** to **$93.4K in 2017**.
* **Technology** delivered the strongest category-level profitability.
* **West** was the strongest region by both sales and profit.
* **Central** recorded the weakest regional profit margin.
* **Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies** were identified as loss-making sub-categories.

## Key Insights

### 1. Strong Overall Growth

The business experienced sustained growth throughout the four-year period, with both sales and profit reaching their highest levels in 2017.

### 2. Technology Drives Profitability

Technology generated the strongest category-level profit and margin, making it an important area for continued investment and profitable growth.

### 3. Furniture Requires Attention

Furniture produced substantial sales but comparatively weak profit. The category's profitability is heavily affected by underperforming sub-categories.

### 4. Loss-Making Sub-Categories Are Concentrated

Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies generated negative profit. These products should be reviewed at SKU level for pricing, discounts, costs, and product assortment.

### 5. Regional Profitability Differs

West is the strongest region, while Central has the lowest margin. Central's relatively high average discount suggests that discounting and pricing strategy should be investigated.

### 6. Discount Control Is a Key Opportunity

The analysis indicates that high discounting is present in several weak-performing areas. More disciplined discount thresholds could help protect margins without unnecessarily reducing sales volume.

## Analytical Approach

The analysis uses:

* Pivot-table aggregation by region, segment, category, sub-category, and shipping mode
* KPI calculations for Sales, Profit, Quantity, Orders, Average Order Value, Discount, and Profit Margin
* Yearly and monthly trend analysis
* Profitability and loss-area identification
* Comparative performance analysis
* Business recommendations based on observed patterns

## Business Recommendations

* Introduce stronger discount approval thresholds, particularly for low-margin products and regions.
* Review pricing and cost structures for loss-making Furniture products.
* Prioritize profitable categories and sub-categories such as Technology and high-margin product groups.
* Develop targeted improvement plans for low-margin regions and states.
* Monitor Sales, Profit, Margin, Discount, and loss-making products regularly through the dashboard.
